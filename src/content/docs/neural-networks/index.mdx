This track constructs a neural network from first principles. We begin with a
single **neuron**, compose neurons into a **network**, then derive the algorithm
by which it learns — **backpropagation** — and observe it train, entirely within
the browser.

## What this track produces

By its conclusion, the reader will have trained a small multi-layer perceptron to
separate two interleaving spirals — implemented from scratch in NumPy, with every
gradient verified against an automatic-differentiation engine.

<Aside type="note">
	The prerequisites are modest: familiarity with vectors and derivatives. All
	remaining material is re-derived as it is needed.
</Aside>
