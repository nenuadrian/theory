The works cited across the site, grouped by track and ordered by year. Each
lesson page also carries its own **References** section listing the papers
relevant to it; this page aggregates them. Foundational works that several tracks
draw on (the transformer, the natural gradient, REINFORCE) are listed under each
track that uses them. Links resolve to arXiv, a DOI, or the primary source.
