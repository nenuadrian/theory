The [neural-network track](/neural-networks/the-neuron/) constructed a model that maps a
fixed vector to an output. Language, audio, and code, however, are **sequences**, and
their meaning resides in *how the pieces relate*. The **transformer** is the
architecture that made the modeling of those relationships scalable, and it now underpins
nearly every large language model.

Its central idea is **attention**: each position is permitted to consult every other position
and to draw in whatever is relevant. There is no recurrence and no convolution — only a learned,
content-based lookup, applied in parallel across the entire sequence.

## What this track builds

By its conclusion the reader will have assembled a complete **pre-LN transformer block** — multi-head
self-attention, a feed-forward network, residual connections, and layer
normalization — written from scratch in NumPy and stacked into a deep network,
with the shape of the residual stream preserved end to end.

<Aside type="note">
	Prerequisites: the [neural-network track](/neural-networks/the-neuron/), or
	familiarity with matrices, the [softmax](/neural-networks/forward-pass/), and
	backpropagation. Attention reduces to matrix multiplications and a softmax, so
	the development relies on material the reader already commands.
</Aside>
